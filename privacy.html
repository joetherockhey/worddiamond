Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

WordDiamond is a free word game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We don't collect, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage: whether you picked light or dark mode, your progress on today's diamond, and your daily stats (games played, wins and streaks). It also keeps a copy of the game's own files so it works offline. None of this leaves your device. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the page, for security and to keep the service running. We can't see those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for how GitHub handles them.

Sharing

Share buttons use your device's own share sheet or clipboard. WordDiamond sends nothing anywhere; you choose what to share and with whom.

Children

Because nothing personal is collected, the game is fine for players of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/joetherockhey/worddiamond.
