How to play

Four five-letter words are locked into a diamond. They share their point letters — the circles belong to two words at once. Guess one letter at a time; you never type a whole word.

Green — the letter is somewhere on the diamond. Every matching cell flips at once. Free.
Yellow — not on the diamond, but one of its alphabet neighbours is. Costs 0.5.
Red — neither it nor its neighbours are here. Costs 1.0. No wrap: A only touches B, Z only touches Y.

The centre badge is a free clue: one letter that is definitely in the puzzle, and how many different letters the whole diamond uses. Fill every cell before you reach 4.0 demerits.
